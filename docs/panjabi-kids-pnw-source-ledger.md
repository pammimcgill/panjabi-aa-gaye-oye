## Verified against institutional sources

### Bellingham 1907
Western Washington University Libraries identifies Washington State Archives' Bellingham Council records, Whatcom County sheriff's jail register, contemporary September 1907 newspapers, and the Seattle Labor History & Civil Rights Project as evidence for anti-South Asian violence in Bellingham. The draft's high-level reference to the 1907 violence is supported, but specific numbers, names, and quoted speech are not yet approved.
Source: https://askus.library.wwu.edu/faq/116404

### Komagata Maru 1914
Parks Canada records arrival at Vancouver on **May 23, 1914**, with **376 passengers** (340 Sikhs, 24 Muslims, 12 Hindus), exclusion through discriminatory Canadian immigration policy, local South Asian organizing and legal challenges, and departure in July 1914.
Source: https://parks.canada.ca/culture/clmhc-hsmbc/~/~/~/link.aspx?_id=2BD417C19474481DB85572425EA71CB3&_z=z
The Library and Archives Canada passenger-list record independently confirms the arrival date and lists Hong Kong, Shanghai, Kobe and Yokohama as departure/embarkation ports in the record. **These ports describe this vessel, not all Punjabi migrant routes.**
Source: https://www.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/discover/immigration/immigration-records/passenger-lists/passenger-lists-1865-1922/Pages/item.aspx?IdNumber=16041
A contemporary 1914 government commission report is available as a primary document; interpret its period language critically.
Source: https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.828609/publication.html?wbdisable=true

## Image and audio policy
Do not embed archival images until rights and attribution are recorded. Do not invent historical photographs, eyewitness quotations or memories. Grandfather is a framing narrator, not an eyewitness. Audio awaits a fluent Punjabi reviewer and a separately authorized voice source. Do not assume permission to clone a voice or publish a family recording.
