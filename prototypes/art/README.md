# Original illustrated PNW review pilot

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 10 October 2026, then converted to WebP (quality 88) for the standalone reader. These are AI-assisted original conceptual illustrations, not archival evidence or validated reconstructions. No historical photographs were used. Historical, cultural, architectural and clothing review remains pending.

Shared direction: premium children's history storybook, hand-inked watercolor/graphic-novel treatment, three detailed panels, narrow ivory gutters, expressive dignified characters, golden ochre/indigo/sage, no lettering, no logos, no fantasy or caricature.

1. `pnw-chapter-1.webp`: imagined early-1900s Punjab village, family considering departure, farewell with small bundle. Family is fictional; not Geha Singh or any documented household.
2. `pnw-chapter-2.webp`: generic steamship voyage, passengers, distant coast. NOT a Komagata Maru reconstruction or universal itinerary. Generated harbor architecture and family mix are illustrative.
3. `pnw-chapter-3.webp`: imagined BC sawmill, carrying cut lumber, meal in plain lodging. No exact named building or logging-camp claim.
4. `pnw-chapter-4.webp`: separate symbolic panels of displaced workers, passengers offshore and supporters organizing. NOT one continuous event or exact victim portraits. Passenger demographics/shoreline must not be read as documentary evidence.
5. `pnw-chapter-5.webp`: explicitly present-day fictional multigenerational family, neighbors sharing food, grandfather with children. Modern clothing belongs to this contemporary chapter.

The old SVG storyboards are retained but no longer referenced by the reader. Generated PNG originals were used to create these optimized project assets. Alt text and visible interpretive captions are in the reader.
