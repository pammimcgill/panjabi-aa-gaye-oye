About our
collectors.

What is collected

Our scheduled collector reads public event metadata and public RSS or structured article metadata. It stores titles, short descriptions, dates, venue names, images supplied as metadata and links to the original publisher.

What is not collected

We do not bypass logins or paywalls, republish full articles, collect private user information, or use unverified social rumors as news.

Corrections and removal

Publishers and event organizers can request a correction or removal at events@panjabiaagayeoye.com.
