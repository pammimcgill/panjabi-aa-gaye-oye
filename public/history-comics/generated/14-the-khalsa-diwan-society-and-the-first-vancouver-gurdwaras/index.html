The Khalsa Diwan Society and the first Vancouver gurdwaras

How Punjabi Sikh workers built community and North America's first gurdwara

Step back to 1906 Vancouver, where Punjabi Sikh workers far from home decided to build something lasting together. Follow along as a community turns hardship into a place of worship and belonging.

1

Crossing the Ocean

Between 1904 and 1908, thousands of Punjabi Sikh men, and a few women, arrived in British Columbia to work on farms and in lumber mills. Many had served in the British Indian Army or traveled through other parts of the British Empire before coming to Canada.

2

An Unwelcoming Place

Life in Vancouver was hard. In 1906, Sikh newcomers faced discrimination, including being evicted from homes and excluded from public facilities. This made the community feel it urgently needed a place of its own.

3

Forming a Society

On July 22, 1906, Punjabi Sikh men in Vancouver formed the Khalsa Diwan Society to support each other. Before building anything permanent, they rented a simple house to use as their first gurdwara, a Sikh place of worship.

4

Raising the Gurdwara

By 1908, the community had built the first Sikh gurdwara in North America at 1866 West 2nd Avenue in Vancouver, designed by architect William Henry Archer. It was built through donations, as South Asian workers across British Columbia gave part of their wages to help pay for it.

5

Heart of the Community

The new gurdwara welcomed everyone with langar, a free shared meal, and gave food and housing to new arrivals. For decades, it also became a meeting place for important causes, including support for India's independence and the fight for voting rights for South Asians in Canada during the 1940s.

6

A New Temple Rises

The Khalsa Diwan Society later helped build gurdwaras in other towns like Victoria, Abbotsford, and New Westminster. By 1970, the original Vancouver gurdwara was sold to help fund a new temple on Southeast Marine Drive, designed by Arthur Erickson, which still serves the community today and opened a museum in 2012.
